    Cookies Policy

    Last updated: 15 March 2025 · This policy explains how Haven Beyond Walls uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.

    Effective: 15 March 2025 Applies to: havenbeyondwalls.com Contact: privacy@havenbeyondwalls.com

    This Cookies Policy describes how Haven Beyond Walls (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on havenbeyondwalls.com (the “Site”). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more broadly.

    Plain-language summary: We use a small number of cookies to make the Site work and to understand which articles are read. We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

    Cookies can be “persistent” (they remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them) or “session” cookies (they are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be “first-party” (set by us) or “third-party” (set by another domain, such as an analytics service).

    2. How we use cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Strictly necessary cookies: These are essential for the Site to function, such as remembering your cookie preferences or enabling basic security features. Without these, parts of the Site may not work.
    • Analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with the Site — for example, which pages are most popular and how long people stay. We use this information in aggregate form to improve our content and user experience.
    • Preference cookies: These remember choices you make (such as language or display settings) to provide a more personalised experience.

    We do not use advertising or targeting cookies, and we do not track you across other websites.

    3. Cookies we use

    The table below lists the specific cookies we may set, along with their purpose and duration.

    Name Type Purpose Duration
    hbw_consent Strictly necessary Stores your cookie consent preferences so we don't ask again on every visit. 12 months
    hbw_session Strictly necessary Maintains your session while you browse the Site (e.g., for form submissions). Session
    _ga Analytics (third-party) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and calculate visit statistics. IP anonymisation is enabled. 2 years
    _gid Analytics (third-party) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish users for reporting purposes. 24 hours
    hbw_prefs Preference Remembers small interface preferences, such as whether you've dismissed a notice. 6 months

    We may update this table from time to time as our use of cookies evolves. Please check back periodically for the latest information.

    4. Third-party cookies

    Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages. For example, we use Google Analytics to help us understand traffic. Google may set its own cookies (such as _ga and _gid) and process data in accordance with its own privacy policy. We have configured Google Analytics with IP anonymisation to reduce the identifiability of visitors.

    We do not control third-party cookies. You can learn more about how to manage them by visiting the relevant third party’s privacy policy or your browser’s help pages.

    5. How to control cookies

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by:

    • Browser settings: Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can usually find these in the “Options” or “Preferences” menu of your browser. You can set your browser to block or delete cookies, or to alert you when cookies are being sent.
    • Opt-out tools: For analytics cookies, you can use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
    • Our consent banner: When you first visit the Site, you may see a banner asking for your cookie consent. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site, or by using the controls described above.

    Please note that if you disable cookies, some features of the Site may not function properly, and your experience may be less personalised.

    6. Do Not Track signals

    Some browsers have a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature that lets you signal that you do not want to be tracked. Our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals, as there is no common industry standard for how to interpret them. We do, however, limit tracking to the analytics described above and do not engage in cross-site tracking for advertising.

    7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
